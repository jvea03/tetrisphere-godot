# App Review: Guideline 2.1 "Information Needed" (8 Oct 2026)

Apple's message (new developer account, little review history) asks for a
screen recording from a physical iPhone plus seven written answers. The written
part is below; it is also in the App Review Notes field. The recording is the
only thing still needed (record on an iPhone from TestFlight build 5).

1. **Screen recording**: attached to the reply (iPhone, latest iOS): launch, the
   crash-landing intro, level 1 walkthrough, a few levels, Shop, a TestFlight
   (sandbox) purchase, Settings > Restore Purchases, an optional rewarded ad.
2. **Purpose and audience**: Egg Escape: Puzzle Break is a casual 3D puzzle game
   for players 13 and over. Critters are stuck inside giant eggs: the player spins
   the egg, aims a falling block and matches three or more of the same piece to
   dig through the shell and set the critter free. Between levels the player
   collects critters, builds up a camp and a spaceship, and plays a Daily Egg.
3. **Access**: no account, sign-in or demo credentials. Open the app; a guided
   first level teaches the controls. The main features are all reachable from the
   bottom bar (Collection, Shop, Home, Ranks, Clubs) and the Home side tiles.
   Ranks and Clubs show "Coming soon" in this version.
4. **External services**: Google AdMob (rewarded and interstitial ads) with
   Google's User Messaging Platform consent form where privacy law requires it,
   Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before ads, and Apple StoreKit for
   in-app purchases. The app has no server and no accounts: progress is stored on
   the device only. No analytics, no sign-in provider, no AI services.
5. **Regional differences**: the app is offered in every territory except the 27
   EU countries. Everything works the same everywhere it is offered; only the ad
   consent form appears where law requires it (for example the UK).
6. **Regulated industry / protected material**: none. The app uses no licensed or
   protected third-party material; its music was generated with a paid Suno plan.
7. **In-app purchases** (19 products, all configured and submitted with this
   version): Shop tab > Featured Sale (Hatcher's Hoard $4.99), Remove Ads / No Ads
   Pass ($4.99, non-consumable), Bundles ($0.99, $2.99, $6.99), Coins ($0.99 to
   $29.99), Materials ($0.99 to $29.99). The Battle Pass ($4.99) is the maple-leaf
   tile on the Home screen. Home also shows time-limited sale pop-ups (Starter
   Sprinkle from level 8; Hatch Day Feast only 26-29 Nov). Restore Purchases is
   Home > Settings. Ads: optional rewarded ads when out of a booster or hearts, and
   an ad after every third win from level 30; the No Ads Pass removes them all.
