POST CAPTION (Instagram / Facebook)
-----------------------------------
I made a second game! 🥚✨

Egg Escape is out now, free on iPhone and Android.

Every level, a little critter is trapped inside an egg. You spin the egg,
drop blocks, match them to pop, and dig a way out. Save them all, collect
100+ critters, and rebuild a crash-landed camp one piece at a time.

I built it from scratch (code, puzzles, critters and all), and it would
mean a lot if you gave it a try, left a review, or shared it with someone
who loves cozy puzzle games. 💛

📱 Search "Egg Escape" on the App Store or Google Play, or tap the link in
my bio.

LINKEDIN VERSION

I'm excited to share that my second game, Egg Escape, is now live on the App Store
and Google Play.

It's a cozy block-puzzle game: spin an egg, match blocks, and free the
critter trapped inside, then collect 100+ critters and rebuild a camp
along the way. I designed and built it myself in Godot, from the puzzle
mechanics and level design to the in-game economy, live events and store
launch on both platforms.

It's free to play. I'd love for you to try it, and any feedback or a
