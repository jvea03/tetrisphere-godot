KEY MESSAGES
------------
Lead with the gameplay. The critters and the camp are why people keep playing.

  1. The gameplay: spin the egg, drop blocks, match them to pop, and dig a
     path out for the critter trapped inside.
  2. Critters: 100+ cute critters to collect and level up, each with a name
     and its own look.
  3. The camp: every win earns coins and materials to rebuild the crash
     site and fix the rocket, one spot at a time.
  4. The feel: cozy, relaxing, no pressure, free to play.

Please avoid: "no ads", "offline", "win money", and any level count beyond
"hundreds".

NOTES FOR CREATORS
------------------
- Show the game in the first second. The spinning egg or a pop is the hook;
  a face-cam works best in a corner or a split screen.
- React honestly. The critter's escape and a chain reaction are the moments
  to react to.
- Keep text short and in the safe zone: away from the top 15% and bottom
  25% of the frame, where the app's buttons sit.
- Keep the game's sound, or keep it low under your voice: the pops and the
  escape sound help.
- Disclose the ad with #ad or the platform's paid-partnership label, as the
  platform and local law require.
- Finish on the name: close on "Egg Escape" and "free". The store name is
  "Egg Escape: Block Puzzle Match" on Android and "Egg Escape: Puzzle Break"
  on iPhone.
