How to play

  • Swipe to turn the egg. Tap it to aim your falling piece, then double-tap to drop it.
  • Match three or more of the same piece to clear them and dig a path through the shell.
  • Open the escape hole to set the critter free. Running out of hearts ends the level, and you can try again.
  • Stuck? Use a booster: Bomb, Any Piece or Rocks. The pause button has How to Play and Replay Walkthrough.

Purchases

  • Bought something and did not get it? Close and reopen the game, then check again. If it is still missing, email us with the date and the item.
  • No Ads Pass after reinstalling or on a new phone: Home → Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: on iPhone and iPad through reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through Google Play.

Your progress

Your progress is saved on your phone only; the game has no account. Deleting the game deletes it, and it does not move to a new phone by itself.

Chests

You earn chests by playing; they are not sold. Each opens to coins and other rewards, and rarer chests are less common (common 60%, rare 30%, legendary 10%).

Ads

Ads are optional rewards (such as a free booster) and short breaks between some levels. The No Ads Pass removes them. Where a privacy law applies, Settings has Ad Privacy Choices. More in the privacy policy.
