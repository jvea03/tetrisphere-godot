Egg Escape: Puzzle Break – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Egg Escape: Puzzle Break ("the game") is published by Little Guy Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device, does not ask you to create an account, and the only information that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service and the app stores' purchase services need to work.

Information stored on your device

Your progress – level reached, coins, boosters, critters, ship parts, chests, streaks, settings and similar game state – is saved in a file on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. The game does not ask for, or store, your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

Leaderboards and Clubs shown in the game are generated on your device; no scores, names or messages are sent to a server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices.

On iPhone and iPad the game first asks, through Apple's prompt, whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising; if you say no, ads are not personalised with your device's advertising identifier. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game also shows a consent form before any ad is loaded and respects your choice. You can change that choice later in Settings, where it applies. If you buy the No Ads Pass, the game stops requesting ads.

Purchases (Apple App Store and Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad and by Google Play on Android. We never see or store your payment details. The store provides the game with a purchase record (product and order identifier) so that the items can be granted and restored on this device. Your payment data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy or the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports

The game does not include third-party analytics. Google Play and App Store Connect may share aggregated install, usage and crash statistics with us; these do not identify you.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit ad personalisation: device Settings → Google → Ads on Android, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iPhone and iPad.
  • Delete all game data: uninstall the game, or clear its data in device Settings → Apps.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently – for example if we add online leaderboards or analytics – we will update this page and the date at the top before the change ships.
