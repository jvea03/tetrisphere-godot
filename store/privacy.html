Egg Escape: Puzzle Break – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Egg Escape: Puzzle Break ("the game") is published by Little Guy Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device, does not ask you to create an account, and does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

Your progress – level reached, coins, boosters, critters, ship parts, chests, streaks, settings and similar game state – is saved in a file on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. The game does not ask for, or store, your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

Leaderboards and Clubs shown in the game are generated on your device; no scores, names or messages are sent to a server.

Advertising and purchases

This version of the game contains no advertising SDK and no real-money purchases: the ad and shop screens are placeholders that reward you without contacting any ad network or payment service. If a future version adds Google AdMob ads or Google Play in-app purchases, we will update this page – including what those Google services collect – before that version is released.

Analytics and crash reports

The game does not include third-party analytics. Google Play may share aggregated install and crash statistics with us through the Play Console; these do not identify you.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete all game data: uninstall the game, or clear its data in device Settings → Apps.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently – for example if we add ads, purchases, online leaderboards or analytics – we will update this page and the date at the top before the change ships.
